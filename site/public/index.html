Frequently Asked Questions

Is Isolate Client free to use?

Yes, Isolate Client is completely free to download and use. There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, or premium features locked behind a paywall. All features are available to everyone.

Is Isolate Client safe to use?

Absolutely! Isolate Client is completely safe and trusted by thousands of players worldwide. The launcher is built with security in mind and contains no malware, adware, or unwanted software.

Does Isolate Client work with Minecraft mods?

Yes, Isolate Client has built-in support for Fabric mods with one-click installation. You can easily install performance mods like Sodium, Lithium, and Starlight, as well as quality-of-life mods, shader packs, and more directly from the launcher.

What platforms does Isolate Client support?

Isolate Client is available for Windows (10/11), (Intel and Apple Silicon), and Linux (Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch, and other major distributions). Download the version for your operating system from our website.

How does Isolate Client improve FPS?

Isolate Client includes built-in performance optimization mods and tweaked settings that significantly reduce lag and improve frame rates. Features include optimized rendering, faster chunk loading, reduced memory usage, and pre-configured performance mods that work out of the box.

Which Minecraft versions does Isolate Client support?

Isolate Client supports all Minecraft Java Edition versions.

How is Isolate Client different from other launchers?

Isolate Client combines performance, simplicity, and customization in one modern launcher. Compared to TLauncher or SKLauncher, Isolate Client offers built-in performance mods designed for faster startup and higher FPS, a customizable HUD menu, an integrated mod menu, an in-game account switcher, a friends system, and cosmetics. With a modern UI, easy mod management, quick access to settings, and a lightweight design, Isolate Client makes it easy to personalize your Minecraft experience—all completely free.
