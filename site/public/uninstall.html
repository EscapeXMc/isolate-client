Uninstall Isolate Client

Clean removal · keep or wipe your data · no strings left behind

Isolate Client is free forever — install it again whenever you like with a single click, straight from the Download page. Uninstalling removes the launcher, not your Minecraft: instances, servers, mods and settings are yours and can be kept, backed up or taken along to another launcher.

1. The cleanest way: uninstall from inside the launcher

Settings → General → Danger Zone → Uninstall Isolate Client The confirmation has a single checkbox, so you choose how much disappears: • Checkbox off (default) — the launcher, its desktop shortcut and Status.txt are removed. Your instances, servers, mods, libraries and settings stay in ~/.isolate, so reinstalling later keeps everything. • Checkbox on — the whole ~/.isolate folder goes too: every instance, server, mod and setting. This cannot be undone. The launcher closes itself and a small detached helper finishes the deletion a moment after the process exits — that is normal, and it is the bare minimum a running app needs to delete itself.

2. Windows

Either run the in-app uninstall above, or open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Isolate Client → Uninstall. The Isolate Client desktop icon and Start-Menu entry are removed with it. Prefer the classic route? Control Panel → Programs and Features → Isolate Client Installer → Uninstall does the same job.

3. Linux — Debian / Ubuntu

Run the in-app uninstall, or from a terminal: sudo apt remove isolate-installer That is the whole command — no root operations leave files behind anywhere else.

4. What gets removed, and what stays

With the default checkbox off, everything under ~/.isolate survives an uninstall untouched. Reinstall Isolate Client tomorrow and your profiles, servers and mods are exactly where you left them. Only tick the full-wipe box if you truly want a blank slate — there is no undo.
